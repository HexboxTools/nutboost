    How it works

    1. Your browser reads the feed's Podcasting 2.0 <podcast:value type="lightning"> block. If the episode has its own block, that one wins.
    2. The boost is split by the feed's shares in whole sats, using largest remainder. fee="true" recipients take their percentage off the top first.
    3. For each Lightning-address recipient, nutboost fetches an invoice for exactly that share (LUD-16/06). The invoice carries an rss::payment::boost comment with your message (LUD-12). Then it asks your mint for a melt quote (NUT-05).
    4. You see the total, including the mints' fee reserves, before anything is paid. Then each share is paid with its own melt. The proofs are journaled before every melt. If a payment fails, the mint is asked whether those proofs were spent (NUT-07). Unspent proofs go back into your wallet.
    5. Ecash-direct recipients skip Lightning. A type="cashu" recipient holds a NUT-18 payment request (creqA…), delivered by HTTP POST or a NIP-17 DM. A type="nutzap" recipient is an npub, paid as a NIP-61 nutzap: ecash P2PK-locked to the key in their kind:10019, with a 30-day refund to you. If they don't accept your mint, nutboost moves the share across by Lightning first (a "cross-mint"), and adds the target mint's input fee so they still net their full share.
    6. Keysend-only (node) recipients can't be paid by a mint. They're shown and skipped, and you keep their share, unless you tick "redistribute".

    Custodial note: ecash is a bearer IOU from the mint you choose. Keep small amounts. Open source (MIT): github.com/HexboxTools/nutboost. Built for Nut November 2026.
